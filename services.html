Expert Gynaecological Services

Comprehensive medical solutions tailored for women at every milestone of their healthcare journey.

Pregnancy Care With Normal Delivery and LSCS

Complete prenatal and postnatal care for expecting mothers, supporting both normal vaginal delivery and Caesarean section (LSCS) with expert monitoring at every stage.

Who It's For All expecting mothers, from routine pregnancies to those requiring planned or emergency Caesarean delivery.
What to Expect Regular antenatal check-ups, growth scans, blood tests, birth planning, painless delivery option, and postnatal support.

High Risk Pregnancy And ICU Faculty

Specialised monitoring and expert care for pregnancies involving diabetes, hypertension, multiple births or other complex medical conditions, with full ICU support.

Who It's For Women over 35 or under 17, those with diabetes, hypertension, heart conditions, multiple pregnancies, or history of pregnancy complications.
What to Expect Frequent antenatal visits, specialised scans, fetal monitoring, ICU and NICU access, and a detailed personalised delivery plan.

Family Planning

Personalised guidance on contraception, family spacing and pre-conception health to help you plan your family safely and confidently.

Who It's For Any woman or couple looking to plan, space or prevent pregnancy safely at any stage of life.
What to Expect Discussion of all options (hormonal, non-hormonal, permanent), suitability assessment, prescription and follow-up.

Infertility Services

Comprehensive fertility evaluations, ovulation tracking, follicular study and personalised plans to support your reproductive wellness journey.

Who It's For Couples experiencing barriers to conceiving after 12 or more months of regular, unprotected intercourse.
What to Expect Detailed blood tests, hormonal panel, semen analysis referral, ovulation tracking, follicular study and ovulation induction.

Abortion Services and Contraception

Safe, confidential and medically supervised abortion care alongside comprehensive contraception counselling in a supportive, non-judgmental environment.

Who It's For Women seeking safe termination of pregnancy up to the legal limit, and those requiring post-abortion or emergency contraception guidance.
What to Expect Confidential consultation, gestational age confirmation by ultrasound, medical or surgical procedure as appropriate, and follow-up care.

Gynaecological Problem and Laparoscopic Surgery

Minimally invasive keyhole surgery for gynaecological conditions — faster recovery, less pain, reduced scarring and shorter hospital stays.

Who It's For Women with fibroids, ovarian cysts, endometriosis, pelvic pain, or those needing diagnostic exploration.
What to Expect Short hospital stay, minimal pain post-op, 2–3 small incisions, faster return to daily activities compared to open surgery.

Pap Smear Testing and Cervical Biopsy, Endometrial biopsy, Pipelle biopsy

Diagnostic cell collection screenings and biopsies to detect abnormal or pre-cancerous cellular mutations early, offering vital preventative healthcare protection.

Who It's For Adult women or individuals as part of regular, recommended preventative medical checkups or diagnostic screening.
What to Expect A fast, painless swab procedure during a routine consultation, with lab results processed and shared promptly.

PCOS and Menopause

Specialized endocrine care focusing on regulating metabolic syndromes, PCOS symptoms, acne, weight, and managing symptoms associated with menopausal transition.

Who It's For Women dealing with irregular menstruation, polycystic ovaries, severe hot flashes, or hormonal mood swings.
What to Expect Comprehensive blood hormone analysis, lifestyle-nutrition counseling, and custom medication programs.

coloscopy and cryotherapy

Detailed magnified visual check of the cervix, vagina, and vulvar walls using a high-powered colposcope to identify and biosampler any suspicious lesions.

Who It's For Patients referred with abnormal Pap smear results or visual cervix abnormalities.
What to Expect A detailed exam mapping cervix tissue changes, taking about 15 minutes, with minimal discomfort.
