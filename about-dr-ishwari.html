About Dr. Ishwari

Consultant Obstetrician & Gynecologist

Dr. Ishwari Subhash Shinde is a dedicated Obstetrician and Gynecologist with over 4 years of clinical and academic experience. She completed her MBBS from the MGM Institute of Health Sciences in 2013, followed by her MS in Obstetrics and Gynaecology from the same institution in 2021.

Committed to advanced medical treatments for women, she completed a Fellowship in Gynaec Endoscopy in 2023. She has extensive training in laparoscopy and hysteroscopy, providing cutting-edge solutions for various gynecological issues.

Dr. Ishwari previously served as an Assistant Professor at MGM Hospital and College in Chhatrapati Sambhaji Nagar, combining her clinical expertise with academic instruction and research, and is now fully dedicated to patient care at Yashodhara Hospital, where she is readily available to provide focused and compassionate treatment.
