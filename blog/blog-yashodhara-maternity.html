Advanced Maternity Services at Yashodhara Hospital

PM
Dr. Pooja Mote (Mule) • June 20, 2026 • 5 min read

Your Safe and Comfortable Maternity Journey in Sambhajinagar

Welcoming a new life is one of the most precious moments in a family's journey. At Yashodhara Hospital, under the expert guidance of Dr. Pooja Mote (Mule), we prioritize safety, clinical excellence, and patient comfort to offer a world-class maternity experience.

State-of-the-Art Labor Rooms & Delivery Suites

Yashodhara Hospital is equipped with modern, fully-monitored labor beds and delivery rooms designed to ensure absolute safety for both mother and child. Our facility supports 24/7 fetal heart monitoring, painless epidural labor support, and immediate neonatal care.

Comprehensive Maternity Setup

  • Painless Delivery Support: Access round-the-clock anesthesiologists for epidural analgesia, making delivery smooth and stress-free.
  • Advanced Emergency ICU: Full critical care backup is available on-site for high-risk maternal conditions, pregnancy hypertension, or gestational complications.
  • NICU Facilities: Dedicated neonatal intensive care support with experienced pediatricians to care for premature infants or specialized baby care needs.
  • Lactation & Postnatal Consultation: Expert guidance on breastfeeding, newborn baby care, and post-delivery maternal exercises.

Personalized Care by Dr. Pooja Mote (Mule)

Every pregnancy is unique. Dr. Pooja provides individualized prenatal plans, nutritional counselling, lifestyle tips, and normal delivery encouraging protocols, ensuring a healthy pregnancy and a safe recovery.
