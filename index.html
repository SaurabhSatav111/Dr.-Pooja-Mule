About Dr. Pooja

Consultant Obstetrician & Gynecologist

Dr. Pooja Mote (Mule) is a distinguished Consultant Obstetrician and Gynecologist based in Sambhajinagar, committed to providing advanced medical care for women. She completed her MBBS from the Government Medical College, Sambhajinagar (2004-09), followed by a Diploma in Gynecology and Obstetrics (DGO) from SRTR Medical College, Ambajogai (2011-13), and a Diplomate of National Board (DNB) from PDSCH, Hyderabad (2014-15).

With a prestigious fellowship in endoscopic surgery, Dr. Mote (Mule) is highly skilled in advanced laparoscopic procedures. Her rich clinical experience is complemented by academic dedication, having served as an Assistant Professor at both MGM Medical College and Government Medical College (GMC).

Frequently Asked Questions

It is recommended that young women have their first gynecological consult between the ages of 13 and 15. This initial visit is mostly conversational, aimed at building comfort and explaining developmental milestones.

Yes, the contraceptive patch is highly safe and effective for most women when used as directed by a physician. However, it carries some minor side effects and risks similar to oral contraceptive pills, so a medical history review is required.

Some medications are safe to take during pregnancy, while others can pose potential risks to the developing fetus. You must always consult with your doctor before starting or stopping any medication during pregnancy.

For most pregnant women, moderate exercise is highly safe and beneficial for maintaining cardiovascular health and easing labor. However, you should discuss your physical activities with your gynecologist to design a safe routine.

Infertility is generally defined as the inability to conceive after 12 months or more of regular, unprotected intercourse. It can result from various male or female reproductive factors, and seeking early diagnostic evaluation is recommended.

Common signs include abnormal vaginal bleeding, pelvic pain, or changes in vulvar skin texture and color. Since symptoms can mimic other benign conditions, any unusual changes should be evaluated promptly by a specialist.

Warning signs include severe abdominal pain, persistent headaches, sudden facial swelling, or a noticeable reduction in fetal movement. If you experience any of these symptoms, you must contact your emergency clinical desk immediately.

PCOS is a hormonal disorder characterized by irregular periods and ovarian cysts in women of reproductive age. In contrast, menopause is the natural cessation of menstrual cycles occurring typically in a woman's late 40s or 50s.
