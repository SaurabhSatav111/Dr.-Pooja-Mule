Pap Smear Testing

A routine, painless cervical screening test to detect precancerous or abnormal cells early — one of the most effective tools in women's preventive healthcare.

About This Service

A Pap smear collects cells from the cervix to test for the presence of precancerous or cancerous cells. It is the most reliable screening for cervical cancer and is recommended every 3 years.

Who It's For

All sexually active women aged 21 and above, or as recommended by Dr. Pooja based on your health history.

What to Expect

A quick 5-minute procedure in the clinic. A small brush collects cervical cells — mild discomfort only. Results within days.

How We Do It

1

Pre-test Advice

Avoid intercourse, douching or vaginal medicine 48 hours before the test.

2

Sample Collection

A small speculum is used to access the cervix; cells collected with a soft brush.

3

Lab Analysis

Sample sent to lab for microscopic examination.

4

Results & Follow-up

Dr. Pooja reviews results and advises next steps if any abnormality found.
