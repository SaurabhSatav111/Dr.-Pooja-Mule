About This Service

Dr. Pooja Mote (Mule) provides comprehensive pregnancy care from the first trimester through delivery and postpartum recovery. Whether you opt for a normal delivery or require a Lower Segment Caesarean Section (LSCS), you receive expert care across 3 hospitals in Sambhajinagar.

Who It's For

All expecting mothers, from routine pregnancies to those requiring planned or emergency Caesarean delivery.

What to Expect

Regular antenatal check-ups, growth scans, blood tests, birth planning, painless delivery option, and postnatal support.

How We Do It

1

First Trimester Care

Confirmation of pregnancy, dating scan, blood tests and supplements.

2

Second Trimester

Anomaly scan, gestational diabetes screening, growth monitoring.

3

Third Trimester

Birth planning, fetal position assessment, delivery mode discussion.

4

Labour & Delivery

Normal delivery or planned/emergency LSCS with full ICU backup.

5

Postnatal Care

Mother and baby recovery, breastfeeding support and follow-up.

Frequently Asked Questions
