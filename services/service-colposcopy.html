Colposcopy & Cryotherapy

An advanced diagnostic examination of the cervix to closely investigate abnormal Pap smear results, with cryotherapy treatment available if precancerous cells are found.

About This Service

A colposcopy is a detailed examination of the cervix using a colposcope — a magnifying instrument with a light. It is recommended when a Pap smear returns an abnormal result. If suspicious areas are found, a small biopsy may be taken and cryotherapy applied to destroy abnormal cells.

Who It's For

Women who have received an abnormal Pap smear result, have HPV, unusual cervical bleeding, or visible cervical abnormalities on examination.

What to Expect

A 15–20 minute procedure in the clinic. Mild cramping possible. A vinegar solution applied to the cervix to highlight abnormal cells under the colposcope.

How We Do It

1

Pre-procedure Advice

Avoid intercourse, tampons or vaginal medications 24 hours before.

2

Colposcope Examination

Magnified view of the cervix using the colposcope instrument.

3

Acetic Acid Application

A mild vinegar solution highlights any abnormal cell areas on the cervix.

4

Biopsy (if needed)

A tiny tissue sample taken from any suspicious area for lab testing.

5

Cryotherapy (if needed)

Freezing technique applied to destroy precancerous cells immediately.

6

Results & Follow-up

Dr. Pooja reviews biopsy results and plans next steps within days.
