About This Service

Infertility is defined as the failure to achieve pregnancy after 12 or more months of regular, unprotected intercourse. Dr. Pooja offers complete care from diagnosis to treatment for women and couples.

Who It's For

Couples experiencing barriers to conceiving after 12+ months of regular unprotected intercourse.

What to Expect

Detailed blood tests, hormonal panel, semen analysis referral, ovulation tracking, follicular study and ovulation induction.

How We Do It

1

Initial Consultation

Medical history review, lifestyle assessment for both partners.

2

Diagnostic Testing

Blood tests, hormonal panel, ultrasound, follicular study.

3

Personalised Plan

Medication, ovulation induction or surgical intervention as needed.

4

Monitoring & Support

Regular tracking cycles, emotional support and follow-up care.
